Welcome to Balaji Inn

A simple stay,
thoughtfully done.

Hotel Balaji Inn is located on Shri Balaji Marg, just off Ayodhya Bypass in Bhopal. The property combines practical room comforts with an easy, convenient location for business and leisure stays.

Rooms are air-conditioned and equipped with flat-screen TVs, writing desks, seating or dining areas, refrigerators and private bathrooms.

Near Minal Gate No. 2 Convenient access to Ayodhya Bypass, local shopping and major city routes.
